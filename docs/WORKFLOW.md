# Workflow: from idea to approved video

Roles: you direct. A planner model plans and reviews; a builder model builds (`docs/MODEL-ROLES.md`). One writer per project folder at a time.

| Step | Who | Output | Stop for you? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0. Kickoff | Planner | Four short questions: outcome, audience, platform and length, what must be said exactly | Yes |
| 1. Script | Planner with `pb-content-craft` | Hook options, script (line / on screen), call to action | Yes: pick the hook, approve the words |
| 2. Record | You | One take per beat with pauses. One mic source. A 10 s test first | |
| 3. Transcribe and cut list | Builder | Local transcript, silences, retakes, a cut list | Yes: mark the cut list |
| 4. Audio A/B | Builder | Two or three short listening files | Yes: say which sounds better |
| 5. Visual plan | Planner with `pb-video-style` | Scene table with trigger words and frames, geometry, QA list | Yes: approve the plan |
| 6. Brief | Planner | `templates/BUILD-BRIEF.md` filled in, your decisions quoted | |
| 7. Draft (540x960) | Builder in Hyperframes | Draft video, QA report | Yes: watch and list changes |
| 8. Final (1080x1920) | Builder | Final video, captions file, cover, report | |
| 9. Review | Planner | Findings with evidence, what was not verified | |
| 10. You watch and listen | You | Approval | Yes: only you approve publishing |
| 11. Posting-day checks | You and planner | Facts re-checked, logo permissions, one private phone upload | Yes |
| 12. Cleanup | Planner | Temporary files listed with sizes, deleted after your yes | Yes |

Rules: at most two full finals per video. No uploads, publishing, logins or paid calls without your explicit yes. Use `fable-mode` for steps 5 to 9.
