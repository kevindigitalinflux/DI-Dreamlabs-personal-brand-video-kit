# Brand interview (the agent asks, you answer)

The agent asks these one group at a time and writes the answers to `brand/brand.json`. Skip anything you do not know yet; the defaults in `brand.example.json` are neutral placeholders.

1. Name, handle on each platform, and who the videos are for (audience, one sentence).
2. Colours: background, text, accent (hex). Which one must logos never sit on?
3. Fonts: do you already own or use specific fonts? If not, the example set is fine (all open licence).
4. Logos: which marks will appear (yours, partners, tools you name)? For each tool or company logo you do not own, do you have permission to use it publicly? If not, the agent uses text instead.
5. Platforms and formats: Reels, TikTok, Shorts, LinkedIn, long YouTube? Which come first?
6. Recording setup: phone or camera, microphone, OBS or phone app, landscape or portrait.
7. Hard rules: music or no music, swearing, claims you must never make (pay, results, health), words you must say exactly.
8. Who approves a video before it is posted, and who posts it?
