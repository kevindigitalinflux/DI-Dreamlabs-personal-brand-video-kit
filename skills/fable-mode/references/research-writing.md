# Pack: research, analysis and writing

- **Question first.** Write the decision the user will make with this, and what answer would change it. Research that cannot change a decision is not needed.
- **Primary sources.** Official documentation, the provider's own pages, the actual data. Note the date of each. Treat memory of fast-moving facts (prices, limits, versions, policies) as a guess until checked.
- **Separate three things** in the output: what a source states, what you infer, and what you could not find.
- **Look for the disconfirming case.** Before concluding, search once for evidence that the conclusion is wrong.
- **Answer, then support.** Lead with the recommendation and the one or two facts that carry it. Offer the rest.
- **For writing:** know the reader and what they should do after reading. the user's scripts use zero jargon and everyday metaphors. Cut any sentence that does not help the reader act.
- **State confidence** plainly and say what would raise it.
